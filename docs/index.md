# Design with AI

<p class="home-subtitle">Build and test prototypes with Studio and Beacon</p>

See a need. Build something people can try. Learn from their feedback and improve it. This guide helps you work with an agent to test ideas in Studio and Beacon.

**You bring the design judgment.** The agent helps plan and build. You decide what the prototype should help you learn, whether the experience works, and what to change next.

## Take initiative. Keep the loop short.

**Act on a useful question.** Notice what is blocking people and choose something you can move forward today. Your design judgment helps you identify the need and make the first idea tangible.

**Get feedback while it can change the direction.** Time-box an exploration to a short session, then show what you learned. Move between building, evaluating, and sharing as often as the work needs. A useful first pass gives the team something to respond to.

**Make progress visible.** Share what you tried, what changed, how much time you spent, and what is still open. Ask for the feedback or help that will move the next iteration forward.

For work customers will see or another team depends on, check in with those people before committing to the change. Each loop should help the team learn together.
